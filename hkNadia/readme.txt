1. Go to 'Treasure of Nadia' in the Library of Steam Client.
2. Click the Manage icon at the bottom right corner of the banner picture. 
3. Choose bottom option property
4. At the left top pane, the label language should have English at the right end.
5. From here, you have 4 choices, English, Spanish, French, Simplified Chinese
6  Choose Simplified Chinese.
7. In the command prompt or terminal,
   goto SteamLibrary/steamapps/common/Treasure of Nadia/www
8. Copy the folder hkNadia of nltGamingTTS here. Open index.html with an editor (notepad or vim)
9. cp -r ~/nltGamingTTS/hkNadia addon .
